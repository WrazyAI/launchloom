## Independent walkthrough offers

Before handing off a change with a meaningful executable browser, API, CLI,
configuration, or integration flow, suggest an independent walkthrough with
one specific behavior it would demonstrate. Wait for approval before running.
The current workflow supports independent evidence review, not browser or shell
execution by the verifier. Say who would collect and who would review evidence.
If the user accepts or directly requests one, load `agent-walkthrough` and
confirm the allowed scope before delegating. Do not repeat a declined offer for
unchanged scope, and do not ask inside a walkthrough verifier child. Supplement,
never replace, mandatory project checks. Suggestions are guidance, not a hook.
