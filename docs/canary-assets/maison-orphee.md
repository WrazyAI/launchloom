# Maison Orphée canary assets

These original assets support the fictional Maison Orphée luxury jewelry pipeline canary. They are not evidence about a real business or real inventory.

## Asset set

- `hero-ring.webp`: wide obsidian, silk, and platinum campaign still life
- `private-consultation.webp`: vertical private design consultation scene
- `orbital-collection.webp`: sculptural pendant and earring collection still life
- `logo.svg`: compact repository-native orbital monogram

## Generation provenance

The three photographs were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on September 17, 2026. Prompts required physically plausible jewelry, restrained editorial lighting, no text, no logos, no certificates, no identifiable people, and no copied real-world brand language. The outputs were visually inspected and encoded as WebP at quality 86.

The monogram was authored as SVG in the repository so it remains lightweight and editable.
