Key finding

The OpenCode Go reference analyzer returned a provider 400 on the Roto-Rooter route, then failed all retries. The full three-route pack analyzed successfully on OpenRouter with the same DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model. The creative approach also has a structural tension: its people-free imagery policy conflicts with plumbing references whose opening subject is a technician or a van. A people-free reference strategy, or a separate analyzer path, is required for this niche.
