An absence, explained

There is no before-and-after gallery.

Every clinic in this category has one, which is exactly why it proves nothing. Results photography is trivially changed by lighting, angle and expression, none of it is verifiable by you, and at best it sets an expectation built on somebody else’s face and somebody else’s skin.

At consultation you will be shown cases genuinely comparable to your own assessment, with the lighting conditions stated. That is worth more than a scroll of strangers.

Questions

Including the awkward one.

Why is pricing per unit and not per area?+

Because per-area pricing hides how much product you are receiving. Two clinics quoting the same price for the same area can be using very different amounts, and you cannot tell which. Per unit is the only figure that compares.

How much downtime will I have?+

It is a column on the treatment menu rather than something discussed at the appointment. Injectables are same-day with possible bruising; a medium peel is five to seven days of visible peeling.

Where are your before-and-after photographs?+

We do not publish a gallery. Results photography is trivially manipulated through lighting and angle, it is unverifiable by you, and it sets an expectation from somebody else's face. Real examples relevant to your assessment are shown at consultation instead.

Will you tell me if I do not need it?+

Roughly one consultation in six ends without a treatment plan. If what you are describing will not be improved by what we offer, saying so costs us one booking and saves you the money.

Who will actually be treating me?+

All injectable work is done by a registered nurse prescriber. Resurfacing and laser are done by our clinical aesthetician. Named on this page, and you will meet them at the consultation.
