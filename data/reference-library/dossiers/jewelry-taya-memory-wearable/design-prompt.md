# Taya wearable-jewelry visual implementation brief

Use the paired desktop and mobile screenshots as visual evidence for composition mechanics only. Create a new, original product story for a client business whose verified product facts and SEO context arrive as sealed content tokens. Do not reproduce Taya's brand, product, language, artwork, photography, testimonials, logo, page copy, or distinctive trade dress. Client assets take priority; otherwise create a new, licensed or generated image set that is explicitly about the client's actual product.

## Visual hierarchy

Lead with an immersive campaign image of the client's real product in use. Keep the product recognizable at the first glance and place a short, factual product promise directly inside the image composition. Give the wordmark and purchase action quiet utility roles along the top edge. Do not build a generic 50/50 text-and-image split. The opening should feel like a single art-directed campaign frame, not a SaaS hero. On desktop, use a wide crop with generous breathing room; on mobile, preserve the image's emotional role while ensuring the product, concise promise, and first action remain legible.

Treat the product image as the opening of a narrative. Follow it with a single-object still life: a pendant, chain, accessory, or equivalent product-specific detail suspended against a clean light field. A thin drawn line, stitch, wire, or other product-relevant graphic may lead the eye into a short editorial explanation. Keep this connector precise and quiet. Avoid ornamental flourishes that have no relationship to the product.

Use an editorial serif for the largest statement and a restrained sans serif for product explanation, technical labels, and controls. Use warm white or ivory surfaces, deep near-black text, and one subdued material accent drawn from client brand facts. Body copy should be short, concrete, and easy to scan. Do not imply product specifications that are absent from the tokens.

## Page sequence

1. Minimal navigation over the campaign image: original client wordmark, compact menu control, and a primary purchase or inquiry action.
2. Campaign opening: use-case image, short promise, and the immediate conversion action.
3. Product connector and editorial introduction: one still-life visual, followed by a concise explanation of what the product is and who it helps.
4. Use-case or film chapter: one larger atmospheric image with a static poster and play control if approved video is supplied. Never autoplay sound; if no approved video exists, use a generated or client-provided still.
5. Product-first explanation: explain the physical design before technical details.
6. A four-part image-backed attribute rail: only include attributes supported by sealed content. Give each a short label, one sentence, and intentional crop.
7. Privacy, care, compatibility, or other decision-critical detail as a separate calm statement, based only on verified facts.
8. Optional proof or testimonial rail, only when genuine approved reviews are provided. Include a static reading-order fallback and visible controls.
9. Compact footer with purchase/contact route, required legal links, and no unsupported claims.

Do not include every source section automatically. Select only the chapters that match this client's product and research. SEO copy must answer real buyer questions using business facts and target terms supplied or independently validated in the SEO context. Keep one clear primary action in the opening and repeat it only at meaningful decision points.

## Responsive translation

At 1440px, keep the campaign image broad and the text overlays short. Let the product still-life chapter breathe as a distinct composition. At 390px, convert the story into a single column: compact logo/menu, full-width image, readable overlay copy, short still-life pause, then text and feature images in a deliberate sequence. Stack feature modules instead of shrinking them into unreadable tiles. Place labels before dense details and make all controls at least comfortably touch-sized. No horizontal page overflow, clipped CTA, or forced scroll-snap is allowed.

Build the source capture's airy rhythm without copying its exact spacing values. Keep transitions brief and non-blocking. A static chain illustration, image, and full testimonials must remain available when reduced motion is enabled. Use semantic headings in order, useful alt text for meaningful images, decorative alt for ornament, visible keyboard focus, and sufficient text contrast over photography.

## Signature elements

- A product-first campaign image that shares one composition with the short promise and primary action.
- A fine-line, product-derived connector between the campaign and editorial explanation.
- A small image-backed feature sequence that explains the product without a wall of generic cards.
- A separate trust or privacy explanation for whichever real buyer concern the verified product facts support.
- A deliberate shift from photographic campaign scale to quiet product-detail scale.

## Prohibited patterns

- No Taya or source-brand name, wordmark, necklace design, exact headline, copy, testimonial, photograph, logo, illustration, or product silhouette.
- No invented price, discount, preorder date, product material, battery life, durability, privacy promise, performance figure, compatibility, warranty, shipping time, or review.
- No decorative technology claims, fake app screenshots, fabricated endorsements, stock person presented as a customer, or unlicensed source imagery.
- No generic split hero, rounded-card wall, repeated accordion stack, autoplay soundtrack, pinned scroll lock, or CTA hidden until the footer.
- No image crop that obscures the actual client product or turns a reference visual into a claim about the client.

Before delivery, compare the render with both reference screenshots for composition and rhythm only. Verify the opening at 100 percent zoom, mobile reading order, current-client asset use, semantic accessibility, reduced-motion behavior, and zero horizontal overflow. All page copy and metadata must remain grounded in verified business and SEO tokens.
