## What is retained and changed

The layout and interaction vocabulary is adapted from Big Picture: viewport-filling, image-backed scroll chapters, alternating side-anchored story panels, a studio/gallery chapter, and a distinct contact close. The provided CSS base was modified; text, structure, navigation, colors, imagery, copy, form, metadata, and interaction code were replaced or rewritten for this fictional neighborhood training concept. Original demo photos, Font Awesome files, Google Fonts import, and upstream JavaScript/vendor libraries were not copied. The five local template SVG graphics under `render/assets/css/images/` are the original Big Picture arrow, overlay, and lightbox controls shipped with the pinned template and remain covered by its CC BY 3.0 license. All displayed photos were generated for this study and are listed with prompts and hashes in `generated-assets.md`.

The desktop/mobile captures are of this clearly modified local adaptation, not screenshots of the HTML5 UP live demo. The screenshot adaptations are stored under the same CC BY 3.0 attribution context as the modified template. This study is not endorsed by HTML5 UP or AJ and must not be used to imply such endorsement.

Visible attribution appears in the study footer and source headers remain in the copied CSS. Changes are identified in this file and in the design prompt. Downstream adaptations must preserve this attribution where the licensed source is distributed or publicly performed.
