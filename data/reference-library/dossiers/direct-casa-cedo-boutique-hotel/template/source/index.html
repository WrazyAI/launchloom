about

Casa Cedo is a thoughtfully designed space for those who seek more than just a place to stay. It’s shaped by care, guided by detail, and open to travellers who value presence over pace. Rooted in the city and inspired by the people who pass through, we create stays that are personal, considered, and intentional. We believe Casa Cedo is about connection. OUR STORY.
A shared dream, realised. Growing up in Italy and Ireland, we were unknowingly connected by a shared love for beautiful places and the art of hospitality. That connection came into focus when we first met, when a late night conversation revealed a mutual dream: to create a home where stories could unfold.

Years of travel followed. We gathered inspiration from around the world, sketching out a vision as we moved through cities and landscapes. It was Porto, with its gentle charm and sense of soul, that called us back again and again. Eventually, we found a building that felt like it had been waiting for us, steeped in history, ready for new beginnings.

Casa Cedo is our labour of love. A sanctuary where elevated experiences meet heartfelt connection. More than a place of beauty or escape, it’s a home for belonging, for acceptance, and for new stories to be written.

We’re so pleased to welcome you into ours.
