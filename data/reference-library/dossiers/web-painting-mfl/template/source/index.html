How We Work

Four steps. No surprises.

We've kept our process tight because contractors hate change orders, homeowners hate guesswork, and nobody likes a vague invoice. Here's exactly what working with MFL looks like.

01

Free Estimate

Call or book online. We do a site walk or plan review, ask the right questions, and return a clean written quote — usually inside 24–48 hours.

02

Schedule & Mobilize

We lock dates, coordinate with the GC or homeowner, pull materials, and show up with the right crew and stock for the job.

03

Prep, Hang, Finish, Paint

Mask, prep, and protect — then hang, finish, and paint to the level your project actually needs. Daily site cleanup. Foreman on every job. No subbed-out crews.

04

Walkthrough & Punch

Final walkthrough with you or the super. Anything that's not right, we fix before we invoice. No nickel-and-diming, no callbacks.

Are you licensed and insured?

+
Yes. MFL Painting is a licensed California contractor — CSLB License #1116782, C-33 Painting & Decorating classification. We carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance on every job, and we'll provide a certificate of insurance on request.

What areas do you serve?

+
We specialize in San Diego County — from Chula Vista and the South Bay up through Downtown, La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe, and the North County coastal cities (Carlsbad, Encinitas, Oceanside). We also run crews into Orange County and Los Angeles for investor portfolios and commercial accounts. If you're in Southern California, we can quote your job.

Do you do interior and exterior?

+
Both. Interior repaints (walls, ceilings, trim, doors, cabinets), exterior repaints (stucco, wood siding, fascia, garage doors), and cabinet refinishing as a stand-alone service. For investor flips we typically do interior + exterior on the same mobilize.

How fast can I get an estimate?

+
For most jobs we can return a written estimate within 24–48 hours of a walkthrough. For commercial bids and multi-unit packages we typically deliver inside a week. Call (619) 997-0165 or book a free estimate online to get started.

Do you work on investor flips and rental turnovers?

+
A big share of our work is investor flips, multi-unit turnover, and pre-listing repaints. We move fast, hit your timeline, and bill clean. Ask us about portfolio pricing if you have multiple properties or a steady pipeline.

What products do you use?

+
Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore are our primary product lines for interiors. For exteriors we spec product to the substrate — elastomeric on stucco, acrylic on wood, DTM on metal. Low-VOC and zero-VOC available on request.
