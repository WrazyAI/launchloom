Welcome to ethos Greek Bistro

Discover Our Story

Authentic Greek & Mediterranean Food - ethos is a Greek word meaning “character” or the fundamental values of cultures, ideas and customs. In short, the distinct spirit of a culture.

Our Greek restaurants have a contemporary ambiance and serve a modern take on most of our dishes that derive from family recipes past down from generations.
 
ethos Greek Bistro’s cooking style is vastly based on the Greek Islands, more specifically, the Greek food native in the island of Crete… situated at the most southern part of Greece and surrounded by the Aegean and Mediterranean seas. Crete, the birthplace of the Mediterranean diet, is considered to be light, mainly vegetarian and seafood-based with less emphasis on red meats and pasta.
Influenced by one of the healthiest cuisines known, the goal of ethos is to showcase our unique take on authentic Greek and Mediterranean cuisine through an exciting array of modernized dishes for today’s palette.
Our meze style plating is best when sampled by all. Reveal your adventurous side by selecting a multitude of our appetizer-like courses.
 
Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options are available. We're proud to be one of the best Greek restaurants in Fort Lauderdale and Coconut Creek. Indulge in the guilt free pleasures of fresh, healthy and high quality dishes.
