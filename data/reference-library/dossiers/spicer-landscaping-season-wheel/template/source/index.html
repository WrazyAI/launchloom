The calendar

Four seasons, four different jobs.

Spring

Mid-March to late April

Everything in spring is timed off soil temperature, not the calendar. Miss the window and you spend the rest of the year correcting it.

  • Pre-emergent crabgrass control before soil holds 55°F
  • Cutback of ornamental grasses and perennials to 4 in
  • Bed edging and 2–3 in of hardwood mulch, kept off crowns
  • First mow at 3.5 in with fresh blades, clippings returned
  • Irrigation start-up, zone-by-zone head check and timer set
If it gets skipped

Crabgrass germinates and you are on post-emergent chemistry all summer. Matted leaf litter left over winter invites snow mould and crown rot in the turf underneath.

Why we ask for photos

Four photographs turn the first site visit into a closing visit instead of a measuring one. We can see whether a 36 inch mower fits the side gate, how much of the lawn is under a canopy, whether the beds have real edges or just a mulch line, and whether that slope needs a walk-behind rather than a rider.

It also means the number we bring is the number you pay. Nobody enjoys a revised price on the day the crew turns up.

What clients say

Three yards, three different asks.

Questions

Asked before every first visit.

If yours is not here, ask it on the call. We would rather lose ten minutes than have you find out something in June that we could have told you in March.

Do you do design or just maintenance?

Both, and they are priced separately so you can take the design and have anyone build it if you prefer. The plan sheet is measured, scaled and yours to keep either way.

What does maintenance actually include?

Mowing, edging, bed weeding and blowing every visit, with pruning and fertilization on a seasonal schedule. It is listed line by line on the plan, not left vague.

How far out are you booking?

Maintenance starts within a week or two. Design-build runs three to six weeks in spring and considerably less in autumn, which is the better planting window anyway.

Can you handle irrigation as well?

System install, seasonal start-up and winterization, plus smart controllers that read local rainfall and typically cut a summer water bill by a fifth to a third.

What counts as seasonal cleanup?

Spring cutback and fresh mulch, rotating leaf passes through autumn, and storm debris clearing whenever it happens. Cleanups are quoted by property, not by truckload.

Do you guarantee the plants you install?

Trees, shrubs and perennials we supply and plant carry a one-year replacement warranty, provided the watering schedule we leave you was followed. Annuals and transplants of your own existing plants are excluded.

What happens if it rains on my service day?

We push to the next dry day on the same route rather than skipping the week, because mowing a saturated lawn does more damage than a two-day delay. You get a text the morning a route moves.
