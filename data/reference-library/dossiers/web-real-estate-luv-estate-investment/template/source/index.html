LUV ESTATE

Remarkable homes, curated investments

LUV Estate is a boutique real estate and investment firm focused on a singular vision: delivering and investing in exceptional assets shaped by LUV.

We exclusively commercialize properties designed by LUV Studio, homes conceived through our in-house architecture and interior design expertise, where design, craftsmanship, and lifestyle come together seamlessly.

At the same time, LUV Estate acts as a strategic investor, sourcing and curating unique real estate opportunities that meet our standards of quality, location, and long-term value.

We work with clients who value design, discretion, and a thoughtful approach to real estate, offering access to a carefully selected portfolio of assets, either created by LUV or rigorously curated by our team. From the sale of LUV-designed residences to bespoke acquisitions and investment advisory, our approach is defined by clarity, trust, and attention to detail.

Our portfolio spans prime locations across Spain, including Barcelona, Madrid, Marbella, and the Balearic Islands. Each opportunity is aligned with the vision and standards of the LUV group.
