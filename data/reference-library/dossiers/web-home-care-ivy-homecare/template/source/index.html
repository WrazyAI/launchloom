Care that feels familiar. Support that feels personal.

Ivy Homecare Services is a family-run domiciliary care provider supporting adults across the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea and Camden. The service helps individuals remain living independently in their own homes by providing flexible, person-centred support designed around personal needs and routines.

About Ivy Homecare

Helping you stay independent in the place you know best

Care is delivered on a visiting or ongoing basis depending on requirements, with support that can be adapted as circumstances change. Services include personal care, companionship, help with medication, meal preparation, domestic support, overnight care, respite care, emergency care, palliative care and 24-hour care.

Care in action

Compassionate support that makes a real difference

Take a closer look at the warmth, dignity and personal attention at the heart of Ivy Homecare Services. Every visit is shaped around the individual, helping each person feel safe, respected and comfortable at home.

Home care services

The right support, at the right time

From occasional visits to continuous care, support is designed around individual needs and established routines.

♡

Personal Care

Respectful assistance with washing, dressing, continence care and daily personal routines.

♧

Companionship

Friendly conversation, reassurance and meaningful company to support emotional wellbeing.

+

Medication Support

Medication prompts or assistance provided according to the agreed care plan and routine.

◌

Meal Preparation

⌂

Domestic Support

Practical assistance with light cleaning, laundry, shopping and essential household tasks.

☾

Overnight Care

Sleeping or waking-night support for safety, reassurance and assistance during the night.

◷

Respite Care

Short-term care that allows family carers to rest while established routines continue.

⚡

Emergency Care

Responsive support when care is required unexpectedly or circumstances suddenly change.

❧

Palliative Care

Compassionate support focused on comfort, dignity and personal wishes at the end of life.

☼

24-Hour Care

Continuous care for ongoing reassurance, comfort and support throughout the day and night.

Who we support

Experienced support for a wide range of needs

The service supports adults with a range of needs, including older people, those living with long-term conditions, disabilities and individuals requiring end-of-life care. Coordination with families and healthcare professionals helps maintain consistent and joined-up support.

✓ Older people
✓ Long-term conditions
✓ Physical disabilities
✓ Dementia and memory needs
✓ Parkinson’s and stroke recovery
✓ End-of-life care
Why families choose Ivy

Safe, reliable care with a human connection

Care staff are carefully recruited and receive ongoing training to ensure safe and reliable delivery of care. A focus on continuity helps build trusting relationships between carers and the people they support.

Ivy Homecare Services is committed to providing practical support alongside meaningful companionship, enabling people to remain comfortable and supported in familiar surroundings.
