Our Story

Roasting Exceptional Coffee Since 2014

Founded in Portland's vibrant coffee scene, we've dedicated a decade to perfecting our craft and building relationships with farmers across the globe.

Sourcing

We build lasting relationships with coffee farmers and importers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Every bean is carefully selected from farms that prioritize environmental stewardship and fair labor practices.

Roasting

Our small-batch roasting process brings out the unique characteristics of each origin. We roast to highlight the natural flavors of the bean, creating a balanced cup that honors the work of everyone who touched the coffee before us.
