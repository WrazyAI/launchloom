About the Practice

Our practice has been recently refurbished with cutting edge equipment in a refreshed and contemporary space. The practice has been transformed but it retains its friendly, family-focused and professional service.

Drop by and say hello. Our reception staff will be happy to assist with any questions you have. We offer a full array of general dental and aesthetic treatments, as well as running regular hygienist clinics.

Our Approach

Good oral health starts with prevention. Regular check-ups and cleaning will save money and stop small problems from becoming bigger issues. Many adults can benefit from the support of the PRSI scheme.This reduces the cost of examination and cleaning visits.

We welcome all age groups and are proud to say that we especially like looking after nervous patients,young and old. A positive attitude to dentistry starts with regular visits at a young age, so children are especially welcome to come for short, simple visits to become familiar to the clinic and the team.

Oral Hygiene

A build up of plaque and calculus around the teeth and under the gums can result in future health problems.

Something that may start with bleeding gums, could lead to gingivitis, or more serious, periodontitis. Poor oral health can lead to bad breath and even tooth loss.

Regular cleanings with the hygienist will help to remove plaque, calculus and biofilm which contain harmful microbes. Seeing the hygienist regularly will help you keep on top of your oral health.

Dentures

Dentures are prosthetic appliances made to replace missing teeth. They are held in by the surrounding hard and soft tissues of your mouth. Dentures are removable. They are a popular, more affordable option to replace missing teeth.

Just like in our aesthetic consultation, our dentist will fully consult you, and provide different options in order to achieve the desired result for you.

Root Canal Treatments

Endodontic treatment saves teeth that would otherwise have been extracted. It involves the removal of pulp tissues from the tooth in the event of irreversible inflammation or infection.

Following this treatment, we will always recommend you get a crown to eliminate the risk of breakage associated with root canal treatments.

  • Incisor €500

  • Premolar €600

  • Molar €800

  • Post & Core Build up €150

Crowns & Bridge

When fillings become larger than the remaining tooth, an aesthetic, yet functional crown can help cover and protect it. We can advise you on the best options here.

Missing teeth can be replaced with a bridge that consists of one or multiple crowns spanning the gap between the remaining adjacent teeth.

A bridge can solve the same problem as an implant, and is an alternative option for patients , depending on their particular needs and circumstances.

Both of these options offer a highly aesthetic yet functional solution.
