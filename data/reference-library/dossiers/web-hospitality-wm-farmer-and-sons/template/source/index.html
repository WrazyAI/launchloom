"The decor is a lesson in restraint. Tasteful relics of Americana—think weathered iterations of the Betsy Ross flag, black and white photos of the William Farmer clan, and amplified Audubon watercolors—pepper spaces where sunlight and rich oak hardwood are spotlighted as the real centerpieces. Beyond Pinterest-worthy, this Hudson inn could very well become the birthplace of the next great American novel—we’re talking serious Walden vibes. A warm, tucked-away check-in area discreetly sets the tone for the low-key, no worries energy sure to define the rest of your stay. With a full kitchen and al fresco garden area on the first floor begging for afternoons and evenings spent entertaining, spacious room sizes that inspire the urge to do cartwheels, and a location smack-dab central to the best of what Hudson has to offer, this family-owned boutique may be small, but its impressive provisions are not to be underestimated." - Condé Nast Traveler
