# Direct-source observations

- Official direct page captured: https://www.dapperdental.com/
- Observed page title identifies Dapper Dental in Winter Park, Florida. The page presents a boutique private practice and several dental service and patient-comfort sections.
- Observed hierarchy: compact dark brand/header; full-width photographic hero with bold outlined/filled display type; repeated oversized statement chapters alternating with photographs and short explanatory copy; treatment/experience modules; testimonial and social sections; final booking and location/contact close.
- The visual language is dark, high-contrast, oversized, and playful, unlike the lighter editorial One Manor Place reference. It can inform an expressive dental route, but its exact content and visual identity are not reusable.
- The live page contains actual practice marketing copy, reviews, clinician references, contact details, and business claims. These are observations about Dapper Dental only, not facts for any other client. Do not copy or re-state them.
- Source rendering issues at capture time: multiple empty image source nodes did not load. An embedded story iframe showed the source host's security restriction in the browser DOM. See `capture-record.md`.
- Only direct-site desktop/mobile captures are included. No gallery thumbnail, image, video, font, logo, script, or other original website asset was downloaded or retained separately.

## Transferable design mechanics

Study the dark image-led opening, high-impact outlined display type, asymmetric alternation between headline-only fields and photography, short editorial supporting copy, productized patient experience modules, and direct booking/location close. For a new dental provider, translate the sequence to confirmed service scope, team identity, location, patient support, and booking method from sealed client facts. Do not reuse the source practice's offers, reviews, claims, staff, images, wording, or trade dress.
