London Chartered Accountants & Business Advisors

Trusted audit, tax and business advisory services

Based in Fitzrovia, London, Goodman Jones has a rich history spanning more than 90 years, supporting entrepreneurial businesses, founders and private clients at every stage of growth. We deliver accounting, audit, tax and advisory services built on rigorous financial insight and strong commercial judgement.

Working closely with businesses and individuals in London, nationwide and internationally, we take the time to develop a deep understanding of their objectives, risks and wider circumstances. This enables us to provide clear, decisive advice that strengthens governance, protects value and supports confident decision‑making.

Trusted independent advisers for over 90 years, offering hands‑on partner support without Big Four pricing

Our approach is relationship‑led, not transactional, grounded in technical excellence, consistency and trust. We help clients cut through complexity, manage risk effectively and plan for the future with assurance.

Through our international network of trusted, like‑minded independent firms, we are well placed to support clients that are expanding overseas or operating internationally. This enables us to advise UK‑headed businesses with global footprints, as well as and international groups establishing or scaling operations in the UK, providing joined‑up, pragmatic support across jurisdictions.

As an independent firm of Chartered Accountants, our clients benefit from direct access to senior advisers, continuity of relationships and advice that is objective and aligned to their needs. 

This allows us to respond quickly, tailor our approach and maintain the culture and values that underpin the quality and consistency of our work.

As a firm, we are committed to enabling our people, our clients and our industry to flourish, supporting sustainable growth built on trust, professionalism and long‑term relationships.

How we can help

I am a business owner

As a business owner, you need an accountant who delivers clarity, confidence, and control. You rely on someone who can simplify financial complexity, keep you compliant, and give you visibility over cashflow, profitability, and future forecasts.

You need proactive advice - not just end of year numbers - so you can make informed decisions quickly. Above all, you value an accountant who understands your commercial goals, spots risks early, and acts as a trusted partner who helps you run the business more efficiently and profitably.

I am a finance director

As a finance director, you need an adviser who brings clarity, control and forward visibility across the business. You rely on someone who can simplify financial complexity, strengthen compliance and give you confidence in cashflow, profitability and future performance.

You also need proactive, commercially grounded insight - not just historic reporting - so you can make timely, well-informed decisions. Above all, you value a partner who understands your strategic priorities, identifies risks early and supports you in driving efficiency, governance and sustainable growth across the organisation.

I am an entrepreneur

As an entrepreneur, you need an adviser who brings clarity and pace - someone who can streamline financial, commercial and tax decisions so you can stay focused on growth. Time is scarce, and you need quick, reliable visibility over cashflow, margins, tax exposures and future runway without getting pulled into operational detail.

You want forward-looking insight, not historic reporting: guidance that anticipates challenges, highlights opportunities and helps you prioritise what truly drives scale. And because your business and personal finances are closely connected, you need joined-up advice that optimises tax efficiency for both.

Above all, you value a partner who understands the pressures of building something fast - someone who works at your pace, reduces uncertainty and helps you grow with confidence and control.

I am a private client

As a private client, your financial needs evolve throughout life. You want an adviser who understands that, family and financial decisions are closely connected, and who can bring clarity, structure and foresight at every stage.

In the early stages, you may need support to manage growing wealth, understand new tax responsibilities and structure assets efficiently as your career, business or investments develop. As life becomes more complex, you want someone who can help you balance competing priorities: protecting your family, planning for major milestones, managing risk and ensuring your affairs reflect your long-term intentions.

Later in life, your focus may shift to preserving wealth, passing it on securely and ensuring your legacy is managed with care. At each step, you need proactive advice that looks ahead, anticipates what is coming next and helps you make decisions with confidence.

You value a trusted relationship: someone who understands your circumstances, keeps things simple and acts as a consistent point of support as life changes. A partner who brings together personal tax, property, family wealth, trusts, international considerations and long-term planning into one clear and joined-up picture.

Above all, you want advice that helps you live well today while protecting what matters for the future. A relationship that evolves with you, your family and your ambitions.

Industries

Property and construction

We support developers, investors, asset managers and construction businesses across the full property lifecycle. Our expertise includes structuring, financing, project appraisals, service charge accounting, capital allowances and group reporting. We help clients navigate market cycles, regulatory demands and the operational and financial complexity inherent in property businesses.

Charities & Social Impact

We support charities, social enterprises and membership bodies with the financial clarity, governance and reporting they need to deliver their mission. Our team understands regulatory demands, funding pressures and the need for transparency, helping organisations strengthen controls, demonstrate impact and plan sustainably for the future.

Creative & Media

We work with agencies, designers, production companies, broadcasters and digital creators, supporting them through project-based revenue models, intellectual property considerations and rapid shifts in industry dynamics. We provide clear commercial and tax insight that helps creative businesses manage cashflow, grow sustainably and invest confidently in new opportunities.

Expanding International

We support internationally expanding businesses as they establish and grow operations in the UK. From setting up entities and navigating UK tax and regulatory requirements to managing transfer pricing, reporting and cross-border structuring, we help overseas owners and finance teams operate with clarity and confidence. Our experience across multiple jurisdictions ensures clients entering the UK market have the financial infrastructure and insight needed to scale successfully.

Financial Services

We advise investment firms, wealth managers, lenders and trust companies operating in a highly regulated environment. Our work spans structuring, reporting, compliance and taxation, helping clients manage risk, demonstrate transparency and maintain robust governance. We understand the pace and scrutiny of the sector and provide the insight required to operate confidently and responsibly.

Industrial & Manufacturing

We work with manufacturers, engineering firms and product-based businesses across complex supply chains. Our support covers costing, stock and inventory control, capital investment, cross-border operations and efficiency planning. We help clients strengthen controls, improve resilience and plan for longterm operational performance in competitive, capital-intensive markets.

Individual

We support individuals, families and trustees with personal, property and investment matters throughout their lives. Our expertise covers personal tax planning, wealth structuring, trusts, estates, succession planning, residency and domicile considerations, and the full range of inheritance tax requirements. We provide clear, joined-up advice that adapts as circumstances evolve, helping clients protect their wealth, provide for future generations and make confident longterm decisions.

Leisure & Hospitality

We work with hotels, gyms, restaurants, entertainment venues and leisure operators to provide visibility over performance in a sector shaped by tight margins, seasonality and operational complexity. We help clients manage costs, navigate VAT and employment tax, understand realtime profitability and plan sustainably for growth.

Professional Services

We advise law firms, consultancies and specialist practices where performance, margin management and partner structures drive value. Our support includes governance, cashflow and working capital management, partner remuneration planning and succession. We bring clear commercial insight to help firms operate efficiently, reward talent and plan confidently for the future.
