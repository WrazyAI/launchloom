# Reference implementation brief: personable growth-led accountant

## Scope and non-copy rule

Use these paired screenshots as a permission-attested reference for the organic curve motif, highlighted display words, dual service taxonomy, local industry routing, and human/team proof sequence. The requester attested to screenshot retention and model-reference use; no owner permission file was uploaded. Do not reproduce Change Accountants’ logo, name, York location, staff, reviews, pricing, client count, claims, photos, or copy. All credentials, local coverage, fees, services, testimonials, and results must come from verified client inputs.

## Visual hierarchy

Keep navigation light and unboxed. Set a strong two-line opening statement with one colored keyword on a cool neutral surface. Pair it with one cropped, approved portrait or client-owned/generated image inside an asymmetric curved aperture. The image and green shape are compositional counterweights, not stock-person hero decoration. Include one primary enquiry action, then reveal the service taxonomy immediately below.

## Page sequence

1. Minimal wordmark and concise navigation.
2. Asymmetric headline-and-image hero with a clear conversation action.
3. Separate service areas by audience or customer need when the practice supports them.
4. Organic color field containing verified industry/service-area links.
5. Useful articles or resources answering visitor questions.
6. Team section from approved staff facts/photos, or a process explanation if no team imagery exists.
7. Genuine, permissioned client proof beside human context.
8. Fixed-fee quote or practical next-step module backed by the actual sales process.
9. Accurate review proof and contact footer.

## Interaction and motion

Use one subtle curve-mask/image reveal; keep service and local links as ordinary anchors. No auto-rotating testimonials, counters, or animated ratings. Do not make decorative shapes click targets. Reduced motion uses static curves and visible content.

## Responsive translation

At 390px, place headline before image and primary action. Keep distinct service groups separate, but translate desktop chips into a readable list. Stack team and testimonial sections, keep enquiry copy short, and protect contact/footer details. Curves must not clip content; verify 320px without overflow.

## Signature elements

Preserve the asymmetrical neutral hero, one highlighted word, organic lime/teal shape language, visible service taxonomy, and the transition from insight to people to enquiry. Use fresh client colors, geometry, typography, and imagery. Avoid small lime text on white.

## Prohibited patterns

No source identity, logo, exact curve shape, staff, photographs, review, fee, credentials, client count, York address, or wording. Do not invent reviews, accounting outcomes, years in practice, client counts, savings, response times, memberships, or fees. Do not imply the client serves listed sectors without confirmation. Avoid turning the organic band into a decorative badge wall.

## Local SEO and conversion

Ground titles, contact details, service routes, and location phrases in verified client research. Publish local service pages only for real areas. Ensure LocalBusiness/AccountingService schema matches current facts. Put one enquiry path in the opening and repeat it at a decision point. If pricing is unpublished, explain the quote process honestly rather than manufacturing fees.

### Type, surfaces and image roles

- Display character: rounded geometric sans with bold colored word accents. Body character: friendly medium-weight sans. Hierarchy: large two-line hero, sentence-case section headings, compact service labels and readable testimonial text. Translate these roles to the host's local font catalog and honor client-selected heading/body families. Specify a responsive scale, line-height and intentional wrapping; keep body lines under about 80 characters. Do not copy source font files or fetch remote fonts.
- Source surface cues: cool light gray; warm white; deep teal. Contrast intent: large lime shapes alternate with teal blocks and white surfaces; avoid small lime text on white. Propose 4-6 named client-compatible color tokens with hex values in the contract, labeled as implementation choices, not measured source colors. Bind them to the host's accessible per-surface palette roles rather than overriding client brand variables.
- Media role: cropped portrait window and candid practice/team scenes embedded in asymmetrical layouts. Crop guidance: wide hero aperture, medium landscape editorial images, simple team portraits. Focal role: human practitioners balance the bright display typography; use only client-owned approved imagery. Map opening/secondary/detail placements to the available client image tokens. Preserve role, focal point and mobile crop without using reference screenshots as site assets. If the right media is absent, use an allowed contextual asset or deliberate graphic treatment, and flag any signature that cannot be met; never imply stock people or spaces belong to the client.
- Service presentation: parallel service taxonomies for distinct customer types, followed by a sector-fit field. Early action: show core accounting service categories immediately after the hero. Primary placement: quote/conversation action in the hero and again at the practical enquiry point. Name the actual client next step; repeat it at decision points, not mechanically after every chapter.
