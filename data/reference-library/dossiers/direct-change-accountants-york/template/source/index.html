Stacey McVeighty

Director / FCCA

Stacey founded Change in 2014 with one simple aim: to build an accountancy practice that changes people’s perception of accountants.

She enjoys solving problems. Whether it’s software that isn’t behaving, accounts that don’t quite add up or helping a business plan its next stage of growth, Stacey loves getting to the root of an issue and finding practical solutions.

With more than 25 years’ experience, Stacey specialises in tax, business strategy and helping owner-managed businesses grow with confidence. You’ll usually meet her during new client discovery meetings, tax planning sessions or as part of our management accounts service.

Outside of the office, Stacey is a keen supporter of the York business community and regularly attends networking events, including hosting her own York networking event, First Thursday.
