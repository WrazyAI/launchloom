faqs

I work with modern B2B marketing teams, usually lean teams of one to five people inside a growing company. Most come to me when the business has moved faster than the brand and website, and there's no dedicated web person in-house. I also partner with agencies and in-house teams who need design or build support.

A full project covers four stages: brand and positioning, website design, build and launch, and an ongoing growth partnership. Strategy comes first, design and build happen together so nothing gets lost between them, and I stay on after launch to keep the site improving. You can take any stage on its own, but most teams run the whole sequence.

Projects that combine design and build typically range from $15,000 to $30,000 and take 8 to 10 weeks. I charge a flat fee based on scope, since every project differs in length, complexity and deliverables. You can book a call to talk it through.

Yes. Every site is built from independent components your team can drag, drop and edit, so new pages and campaigns don't need a developer or an engineering ticket. That's the point of the build: your team ships on its own timeline, not someone else's.

Launch is the start of the growth partnership, where I work as an extension of your marketing team. That covers campaign and landing pages, conversion optimisation, SEO and AEO, analytics and iterative design, so the site gets better every month instead of quietly going stale. Most clients continue on a monthly retainer, and you can book a call to talk about what that would look like.

Both, and messaging usually comes first. A well-designed site built on unclear positioning just looks good while underperforming, so I work through the story before any visual design starts. That includes the harder questions: which audience to lead with when you serve more than one, and how to settle internal disagreement on positioning with structured sessions rather than guesswork.

Yes, just me. Strategy, design and build all run through one person, so nothing gets lost in handoffs and you're never re-explaining context to someone new. If a project needs specialist skills outside my core offering, like copywriting or motion design, I bring in trusted contractors, but I stay the one steering the work. I run weekly check-ins so you're never wondering what stage things are at.
