New chapter begins

Brixton Cycles enters an exciting new chapter under fresh stewardship, led by Angus, a local business owner with a deep commitment to keeping cycling culture alive in the area. Having established his own business in November 2022 and taking over the Harbour Cycles location on Coldharbour Lane in February 2023, Angus has already become a familiar face in Brixton's cycling community.

When news broke that Brixton Cycle Co-op would be closing its doors, stepping aside simply wasn't an option. Determined to preserve its legacy, Angus and his team successfully acquired the intellectual property of Brixton Cycles in July 2025, ensuring the name and spirit could continue.

Co-op → Collective

One of the biggest shifts in this new era is the move from a co-op model to a collective. While a co-op is typically member-owned and democratically run, a collective keeps that same community-driven ethos but allows for a more flexible structure — bringing together passionate individuals who collaborate, share responsibility, and work toward a common goal: keeping Brixton's cycling scene thriving.

Where to find us

Our intention is clear — Brixton Cycles is here to stay, with plans to establish a permanent home in Brixton in the coming years. For now, you'll find us operating alongside Harbour Cycles on Coldharbour Lane, just a little further down from the original Brixton Cycles location.

We're incredibly proud to carry the name forward. Expect a growing range of new brands, a fully stocked selection of parts and accessories, and the same expert service and repairs the community has always relied on.

Brixton Cycles lives on — and this is just the beginning.
