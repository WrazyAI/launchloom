In-home veterinary care

Mobile veterinarian in Washington, DC

Perry Paws Mobile provides personalized, in-home veterinary care for dogs and cats in DC, Maryland suburbs and Northern Virginia. Led by Dr. Dasha Perry, our mobile veterinary service brings compassionate, high-quality care directly to your home, helping pets feel calmer and making veterinary visits easier.

New patients welcome. Our team confirms availability for your address before booking.

Request a visit ↗Explore our services
Serving DC, Maryland suburbs and Northern Virginia. Travel fees apply to visits in Maryland suburbs and Northern Virginia. Please reach out to confirm availability and the travel fee for your address.

We are proud to be certified Fear Free®.

Whether your pet feels anxious at the clinic, you have multiple pets, transportation is difficult, or you simply want a more convenient and personalized experience, Perry Paws Mobile offers a gentle, practical, concierge-style alternative to the traditional veterinary office visit.
