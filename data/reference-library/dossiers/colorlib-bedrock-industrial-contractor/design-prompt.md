# Reference implementation brief: Bedrock industrial contractor

## Scope and non-copy rule

This reference is a local render of Colorlib's Bedrock template at a pinned MIT commit. The MIT notice and per-image CC0/Public Domain credits are retained beside the captures. Re-author the name, copy, image selection, project data, metrics, credentials, and contact details for the client. Do not carry over the demo company, locations, projects, revenue or employee numbers, accreditations, or tender facts.

## Visual hierarchy

Begin with a thin technical utility bar and a very large stacked industrial headline over a wide construction photograph. Use charcoal surfaces, cool-white display type, a restrained yellow signal, and compact metadata. Follow the hero with a metric ribbon only when verified figures exist. The page should feel like a contractor's project fieldbook rather than a generic corporate brochure.

## Page sequence

After the opening, show a concise capabilities grid, a sector or project-type ledger, selected work, a process timeline, factual credential proof, concise FAQs, and a tender/contact form. Keep the tender action visible in the header and hero. If the client has no portfolio imagery, replace the project grid with an honest process or materials chapter rather than fabricated completed work.

## Interaction and motion

Use only light native interactions: in-page navigation, one real theme control if it improves contrast, and ordinary case-study links. A tender form may reveal only the optional fields that are actually needed. Avoid animated counters and parallax over construction photography.

## Responsive translation

On mobile, use a single-column sequence: promise and request, capabilities, sectors, project proof, process, then contact. Scale the poster type before changing its order. Convert wide ledgers to stacked rows and keep project crops recognizable. Verify at 390x844 and 320px with no horizontal overflow.

## Signature elements

Preserve the industrial poster, compact utility navigation, factual sector ledger, evidence-led project gallery, and tender-focused close. Use a dark surface hierarchy with one precise yellow action accent.

## Prohibited patterns

Never invent contract values, customer names, certifications, team size, turnover, completion dates, or project outcomes. Avoid stock construction imagery presented as client work, fake metrics, glossy SaaS cards, and decorative process numbering.

## Local SEO and conversion

Use researched service names, commercial/residential distinction, and verified coverage areas in the sector/service sections. Put the tender or site-assessment request near the opening. All phone, email, address, project images, testimonials, qualifications, and structured data must come from sealed client tokens.
