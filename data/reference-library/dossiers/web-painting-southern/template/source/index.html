Interior Painting

From helping you choose the best colors to meticulously prepping your walls and applying paint with an expert hand, Southern Painting has all your interior painting needs covered. Plus, at the end of each work day, we will clean up and remove all supplies, tools, and trash. We will also put the furniture back where it belongs, rehang shades and blinds, and replace all switch plates and outlet covers. When we leave, your room will be ready for you to enjoy!

Learn More
Exterior Painting

Our exterior painting service sets us apart. Before we begin to paint, we’ll power wash your house, scrape away loose paint, make minor repairs, and apply primer and caulking – all part of our Five-Step Exterior Process. Then we’ll paint or stain your home using only top-quality products.

Learn More
Custom Painting & Finishes

Are you looking to match your paint to a fabric color? Do you want your paint to look like wallpaper or sport a color-washed finish? We can help create the one-of-a-kind look you’ve been dreaming of with services that include texturing, stenciling, mural work, and more.

Learn More
Drywall, Trim & Repair Services

Sometimes a paint job requires more than just a few coats of paint. Are you adding a room? Knocking out a wall? Transforming the garage into your new game room or family retreat? We can help by hanging drywall, installing doors, doing trim work, and more.

Learn More
Southern Painting Deck & Fence Staining

Give your wood fence or deck a fresh appearance and help extend its life by having it restained. Our experts can help you select the best stain to cover the existing finish and provide the look you want. We’ll also do all the prep work.

Learn More
Southern Painting Wrought Iron Fence Painting

Let the experts at Southern Painting restore the luster of your ironwork fence! We can help bring your fence back to its former glory, as well as protect it against continued weathering. We’ll also do any necessary prep work, including rust treatment, before we paint.
