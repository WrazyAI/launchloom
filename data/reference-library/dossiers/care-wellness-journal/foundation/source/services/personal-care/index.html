Before you reach out

Practical questions, answered.

How do we know which type of support to request?+

You do not need to decide alone. Describe the routines that are difficult now, and the team can explain which available services may fit.

Can our family's priorities be part of the care conversation?+

Yes. The first discussion is designed to include the person's preferences and the family's practical concerns before a plan is proposed.

What should we prepare before the first call?+

A short list of daily routines, current concerns, preferred timing, and the people involved in decisions is a useful place to start.
