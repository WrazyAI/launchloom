Do not infer a physical office from a service area, turn stock people into staff, fabricate outcomes, or paste a source menu or service list into a new site. Avoid decorative grids that flatten the auto repair visitor journey, faint text over busy images, inaccessible controls, and hidden content that depends on animation. Never put an em dash in generated client copy. The screenshots are internal evidence, not client-site assets.
