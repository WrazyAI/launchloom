What We Do

Every service your car needs.

MOT & Servicing

🔧

MOT Testing

⚙️

Full Car Service

Oil, filters, fluids, brakes, belts and more: all checked to manufacturer spec and documented clearly.

🔁

Interim Service

Mid-year oil and filter change with safety checks. Ideal for higher-mileage drivers or stop-start city driving.

📋

Vehicle Health Check

Pre-MOT or pre-purchase inspection. We check all key safety points and give you a clear, honest report.

Brakes & Steering

🛑

Brake Pads & Discs

We measure before replacing: pads, discs, calipers. Only what genuinely needs doing, shown to you first.

🔩

Steering & Suspension

Pulling to one side or a vague steering feel? We inspect and repair the full system, front and rear.

🎯

Wheel Alignment

Laser alignment done properly, with before-and-after readings. Saves tyre wear and improves fuel economy.

Engine & Electrics

🔍

Engine Diagnostics

Warning light on? Full electronic diagnostics to pinpoint the exact fault before any work begins.

💨

DPF Cleaning & Exhaust

DPF warning common in East London stop-start traffic. We clean, diagnose, and repair: no needless replacements.

🔋

Battery Testing & Replacement

Dead battery or slow start? We test and replace across all makes. Same-day fitting in most cases.

Quick Fits & Comfort

❄️

Air Conditioning

Re-gas, leak detection, and A/C servicing. Done properly: not just topped up and forgotten.

💡

Bulbs & Wiper Blades

Headlights, fog lights, rear bulbs, wiper blades: fitted while you wait. Fast and keeps you legal.

🪟

Windscreen Chip Repair

Chip repaired before it spreads into a crack. Quick resin fix: usually cheaper than your insurance excess.

🌦️

Seasonal Check

Winter or summer ready-check: battery, antifreeze, wipers, lights. Thirty minutes, fairly priced.
