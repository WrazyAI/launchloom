Questions

Five of them.

Do I need to book?+

No. Walk-ins are the default and most people never book. Saturday after eleven is the one reliable time the queue gets long enough that booking is worth it.

How accurate is the wait time?+

It is chairs occupied multiplied by average service length, updated through the day. It is an estimate rather than a promise, and it is usually within ten minutes.

Can I ask for a specific barber?+

Yes, and the chair strip shows who is in today. Asking for someone specific will normally add to the wait, and everybody here can do everything on the list.

Do you take card?+

Card, phone and cash. There is no minimum and no card surcharge.

Why is the wet shave booking only?+

It takes forty-five minutes and needs the chair reclined and the towels prepared. Turning up hoping for one usually means being turned away.

If you must

Booking, for wet shaves and Saturdays.

Everything else is walk-in and always will be. Turning up is genuinely the faster option most of the week.
