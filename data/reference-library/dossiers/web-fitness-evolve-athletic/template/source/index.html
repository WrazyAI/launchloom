Built for what you do outside the gym.

Evolve Athletic is a gym designed around General Physical Preparation. We build the Strength, Conditioning, and freedom of Movement, that gives you confidence in your baseline fitness, so you can step into whatever physical endeavour you choose to pursue — a weekend hike, a ski tour, a bouldering project, a surf trip, your first race…

We deliver attentive coaching, programmed sessions, and capped class sizes, as well as the freedom to follow your own path in our Open Gym sessions, with a Coach on hand if you want one.

Three ways to train.

Most members mix them…a few classes a week, open gym sessions in between and PT when you want a focused push.

01 — Classes

Coached Small Group Sessions

Programmed Strength Training, Conditioning Circuits, Movement & Mobility, and Calisthenics, all geared to build General Physical Preparedness and well rounded fitness.

Classes are capped to 8 people so the Coach can actually Coach, and you get the most out of each training session.

1:1 Coaching enquiry

Tell us what you're working on.
