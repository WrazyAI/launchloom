aBOUT studio

Indie Interiors is a London-based luxury interior design and architecture studio working across London, the UK and internationally. We specialize in large-scale residential projects, combining considered design with the technical expertise and project coordination required to deliver exceptional homes.

From substantial family residences and country homes to prime central London apartments and townhouses, our team provides a complete interior design, procurement and installation service tailored to each client, property and way of living.

DISCOVER Our Studio

interior design, architecture & procurement

Our approach goes far beyond the decorative elements of a home. From early space planning and interior architecture through to lighting, kitchens, bathrooms, bespoke joinery and FF&E, our team considers how every detail works together.

We then manage sourcing, procurement and installation, coordinating the many moving parts involved in delivering a complex residential interior. this end-to-end approach gives our clients one experienced team to guide the project from initial concept through to completion.

designed around your home and how you live

Every project begins with understanding the property, the people who will live there and what they want the finished home to achieve. Our work spans large-scale renovations, new-build residences, country homes and prime London properties, with each interior developed specifically for the client rather than around a prescribed house style.

Working alongside architects, contractors, specialist suppliers and craftspeople, we balance creative direction with the technical details required to take ambitious residential projects from plans on paper to fully realised homes.
