Questions

What is mckp.live?

mckp.live is a browser tool for animated, interactive 3D device mockups. Drop a screenshot or a screen recording onto a 3D iPhone, iPad, MacBook or Android phone, then export a video or embed the live mockup on your website. It is made by ls.graphics, who have been making mockups for designers for 14 years.

What is the difference between Studio and Origin?

Origin is a library of 389 ready-made animated scenes, lit and shot by designers: pick one, add your screen, export or embed it. Studio is where you make the motion yourself — keyframes for the camera and the device on a timeline — starting from one of 55 presets.

Which devices are available?

Origin scenes: iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone Air, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16e, MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, MacBook Neo, MacBook, Nothing Phone (3) and iPad Pro. Studio mockups: Studio Display, iPad Pro, Pixel Fold, iPad Pro KeyBoard, iPhone 18 Pro, MacBook Pro, iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro in Hand. New devices are added regularly.

Can I put a video on the screen?

Yes. A screen takes an image or a video up to five minutes long. In an embed the video streams, so it starts playing quickly.

How do I embed a mockup on my website?

Copy the embed snippet from the editor and paste it into Framer, Webflow or any HTML page. The scene can play on load, loop, or start on click, hover, scroll or when it comes into view, and the camera can follow the cursor. A light version appears right away while full textures load.

What can I export?

Videos and images at any size and aspect ratio. Scenes with a transparent background export as WebM with real alpha, so the clip sits on top of any page or edit. Long or heavy videos render on our GPU servers, so you don't have to keep the tab busy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. mckp.live runs in the browser. There is also a plugin for Framer.

Is it free?

You can start for free, no card required. Free forever with 3 projects and a watermark. Pro removes the watermark and the limits: $19/month, or $8.25/month billed yearly ($99). Prices exclude VAT. Details: https://mckp.live/pricing/
