Before any colour

Four steps, and none of them are optional.

Colour cannot be booked straight from this page and that is deliberate. What is achievable depends on what your hair has already had done to it, and the patch test is a real allergy risk rather than paperwork.

Reactions develop after years of uneventful colouring. That is precisely why the test is every time, not once.

01

Consultation, free, 20 minutes

We look at what your hair has had done to it before, not just what you want next. Bring photos.

02

Patch test, 48 hours minimum

A genuine allergy risk rather than a formality. It cannot be waived however long you have coloured for.

03

Strand test if there is history

Box dye, henna or a previous correction changes how colour lifts. Better to find out on one strand.

04

Then we book the appointment

With a realistic time on it, so you are not trying to leave at hour three of four.

Cut & finish

Questions

Asked at the basin, usually.

Why does the price depend on the stylist?+

Because experience is what you are buying. A senior stylist is faster and more predictable on complex work; new talent is excellent value on a straightforward cut. Both are listed so you can choose rather than be assigned.

Do I really need a patch test?+

For any colour service, yes, at least 48 hours before. It is a genuine allergy risk, not a formality, and we cannot waive it however many times you have coloured elsewhere. Reactions develop after years of uneventful use.

How long will I be here?+

Every service on the menu has a time next to it. A full head of foils with a cut is around four hours, and we would much rather you knew that before arriving than at hour two.

Can you fix colour from somewhere else?+

Usually, and it is director-level work. It is never quoted over the phone — what is achievable depends entirely on what is already on the hair and how it was applied.

What if I do not like it?+

Tell us in the chair, or within seven days. Adjustments in that window are free. That is a policy rather than a favour.
