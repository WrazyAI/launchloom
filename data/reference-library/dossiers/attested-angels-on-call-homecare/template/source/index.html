Award-Winning Homecare Services for Dementia, Parkinson’s, and Aging in Place in New York

Most homecare agencies are designed to assist with basic daily tasks. Angels on Call Homecare was built to deliver advanced, specialized homecare services for individuals living with Dementia, Alzheimer’s disease, Parkinson’s disease, and other complex, progressive conditions.

We are an award-winning homecare provider serving families across New York, recognized for clinical excellence, workforce development, and client experience. Our care model is designed for situations where consistency, structure, and clinical awareness directly impact safety, outcomes, and quality of life at home.

As the First Healthcare Organization in New York Accredited in Dementia Care and among the first Home Care Providers in the Nation, and the First Homecare Agency in the U.S. Accredited in Certified in Parkinson’s Disease Care (CPDC), our foundation is built on specialized expertise—not general support.

Our Care Professionals receive advanced, ongoing training in Dementia care and Parkinson’s care, including our proprietary Memory Care Angels (MCA) and Neuro-Mobility Angels (NMA) programs. Our MCA program has been recognized as the Best Employee Training Program in the nation, reinforcing our commitment to delivering highly trained, clinically prepared Care Professionals in the home.

Care is further supported by RN Case Managers and a clinical leadership team with advanced certifications in Dementia and Parkinson’s care, working within a coordinated care ecosystem that collaborates with physicians, therapists, and families to ensure continuity, safety, and proactive care planning.

As an Age-Friendly Health System—Committed to Care Excellence, and a Community Partner in Parkinson’s Care (CPPC) with the Parkinson’s Foundation, our services align with the highest standards in healthcare and aging in place.

This is not task-based homecare.

This is structured, specialized, clinically informed homecare—designed for individuals and families navigating complex care needs at home.

A Higher Standard of Homecare—Built for Dementia, Parkinson’s, and Complex Care

Advanced Dementia & Memory Care (Accredited)

As the first healthcare organization in New York Accredited in Dementia care, our approach goes beyond basic support. We deliver structured, engagement-driven care designed to reduce confusion, manage behaviors, and preserve identity and purpose.

Every Care Professional receives advanced Dementia-specific training, including our proprietary Memory Care Angels (MCA) program, with continued education reinforced throughout their time with us.

Parkinson’s Disease Expertise (CPDC + CPPC)

As the first homecare agency in the U.S. accredited in Certified in Parkinson’s Disease Care (CPDC) and a Community Partner in Parkinson’s Care (CPPC) with the Parkinson’s Foundation, we provide highly specialized support aligned with mobility, safety, and disease progression.

Our Neuro-Mobility Angels (NMA) training equips Care Professionals with the knowledge and techniques required to support individuals living with Parkinson’s with precision and confidence.

RN Case Management & Clinical Leadership

Our RN Case Managers and clinical leadership team hold advanced certifications in Dementia and Parkinson’s care and actively oversee every care plan.

We coordinate across the entire care ecosystem—collaborating with physicians, therapists, discharge planners, and families—to ensure continuity, reduce risk, and proactively adapt care as needs evolve.

Highly Trained Care Professionals

Every Care Professional at Angels on Call undergoes advanced training during onboarding and continues with ongoing annual education focused on Dementia care, Parkinson’s care, safety, and engagement.

This includes proprietary training programs, condition-specific education, and real-world application—ensuring our team is not only compassionate, but prepared.

Personalized Matching & Continuity

We match each client with a Care Professional based on personality, preferences, and care needs—creating stronger relationships, greater consistency, and a more stable care environment.

Because in complex care, familiarity is not a luxury—it’s a necessity.

Comprehensive Homecare Services Designed for Real Life

Dementia & Alzheimer’s Homecare

Specialized, structured care focused on engagement, behavioral support, and cognitive stability—designed to improve quality of life for individuals living with Dementia.

Parkinson’s Homecare Services

Targeted care supporting mobility, fall prevention, routine, and neurological changes—delivered by Care Professionals trained specifically in Parkinson’s care.

Live-In Care Services

24-hour care providing consistency, safety, and a stable daily rhythm—ideal for individuals who require ongoing supervision and support.

Couples Care

Support designed for couples who wish to remain together at home, ensuring both individuals receive personalized care while maintaining their shared environment and independence.

Companion Care

Meaningful social interaction, emotional support, and assistance with daily routines that promote connection, engagement, and overall well-being.

Personal Care Assistance

Hands-on support with bathing, dressing, hygiene, mobility, and activities of daily living—delivered with dignity and respect.

Skilled Nursing & Case Management

Clinical oversight, care coordination, and higher-level support for individuals with more complex medical needs.
