Do not infer a physical office from a service area, turn stock people into staff, fabricate outcomes, or paste a source menu or service list into a new site. Avoid decorative grids that flatten the hospitality visitor journey, faint text over busy images, inaccessible controls, and hidden content that depends on animation.
