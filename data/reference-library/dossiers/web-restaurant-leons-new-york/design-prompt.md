# Prohibited patterns

Do not reuse Leon's's logo, wording, photographs, staff or customer imagery, reviews, awards, credentials, rates, exact colors, exact typeface pairing, or overall trade dress. Do not infer a physical office from a service area, turn stock people into staff, fabricate outcomes, or paste a source menu or service list into a new site. Avoid decorative grids that flatten the restaurant visitor journey, faint text over busy images, inaccessible controls, and hidden content that depends on animation. Never put an em dash in generated client copy. The screenshots are internal evidence, not client-site assets.

# Local content and verification

Use the new client's confirmed services and geography in title, H1, useful headings, FAQs, and contact route. A displayed phone number, booking link, hours, price, review, guarantee, accreditation, or service promise must come from the client or another approved fact source. Verify the rendered desktop and mobile page. The final page should clarify scope, preparation, process, and next action.
