At HP Homecare, we believe that exceptional care begins with a clear, transparent process. Here's how we work with you to deliver the best possible outcomes:

1

Initial Consultation

We start by listening. During your initial consultation, our care specialists will discuss your needs, preferences, and goals. This helps us understand how we can best support you and your family.

2

Comprehensive Assessment

Our expert team conducts a thorough assessment, evaluating medical, emotional, and lifestyle needs. This ensures we create a care plan that's tailored to your unique situation.

Personalised Care Plan

Based on the assessment, we develop a fully personalised care plan. Whether you need 24-hour nursing care, post-hospital support, or specialist dementia care, we'll design a programme that meets your needs.

We regularly review your care plan to ensure it continues to meet your needs. If adjustments are required, we'll make them promptly to ensure the best possible outcomes.

Is 24-hour care the same as live-in care?

No. Our 24-hour home care services involve two carers or nurses working in shifts, so someone is always awake and alert. Live-in care typically includes rest periods and is better suited to lower-dependency needs. We'll help you decide which option is right. → Read more about our 24-hour home care services in London.

How quickly can care be arranged?

We can often begin within 24–48 hours, including nurse-led packages for hospital discharges or urgent situations. Our in-home assessment ensures a safe, high-quality fit from day one. Meet our trusted private home care team in London and discover our exceptional qualifications.

Can I meet the carer before they start?

Yes. You'll be directly involved in choosing your carer or nurse. Every member of our team is highly vetted and matched not just to clinical needs, but to personality and lifestyle. Where possible, if you're in hospital awaiting discharge, our registered nurse manager may be able to introduce a member of the team in person to make for a very smooth transition to home. Specialist dementia care at home and other medical services are available.

Do you provide medical as well as personal care?

Absolutely. Our care spans from companionship and daily living support to complex medical needs. Our registered nurse managers can provide services such as injections, wound care, medication management, catheter care, and all procedures you would expect from a nurse—think of it like a private district nurse service. → Learn about our private district nurse service. → Learn more about our specialist dementia support at home. Read our complete guide to private home care and service options.

Can I use private homecare instead of live-in or nursing home care?

Yes. Private homecare allows you to stay in familiar surroundings while receiving personalised support—ranging from daily living assistance to full-time nursing care. We tailor each plan to your needs and comfort, ensuring flexibility and dignity in a homely environment.

Will your home health care nurses handle complex medical procedures at home?

Absolutely. Our experienced registered nurses are skilled in services such as catheter care, medication management, wound dressing, injections, and post-operative support. We work closely with your GP or specialists to create a private home health care plan that meets all medical needs—right in your own home.
