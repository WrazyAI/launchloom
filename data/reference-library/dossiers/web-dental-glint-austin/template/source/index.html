Meet the doctor

Dr. Iana
Elledge, DMD

Founder of Glint. Born and raised in Ukraine, trained at Tufts, and shaped by several years at the Kois Center in Seattle — one of the most rigorous postgraduate programs in dentistry.

She moved to Austin with a vision and zero interest in cold, clinical dentistry. Glint is what came of it: aesthetic excellence and whole-body wellness in one room. She calls it positive dentistry — patients just call it the first time they haven’t dreaded the chair.

Advanced care

World-class smiles,
through innovation.

A boutique studio paired with the technology of a specialty clinic. Every case is scanned, designed, and previewed digitally before a single permanent change is made.

01

iTero Element scanner

A 3D digital scan of your teeth in minutes — no trays, no impression material, no gagging.

02

Digital smile design

Your proposed result rendered before treatment, so you approve the design rather than trust it.

03

CEREC CAD/CAM milling

Ceramic crowns, veneers and onlays fabricated in studio, often in a single visit.

04

Sirona cone beam CT

Full 3D diagnostics for implant planning and complex reconstruction — certainty instead of estimation.
