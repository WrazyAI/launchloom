Questions

The four we get every week.

Will servicing here void my warranty?+

No. A manufacturer cannot require dealer servicing to keep a factory warranty valid, provided the correct parts and intervals are used. We service to the manufacturer schedule and give you documentation that records it.

Why is there a diagnostic fee?+

Because diagnosis is the work. A scan tool reads a code in thirty seconds; finding what caused it takes a technician, a road test and often a scope. The fee is credited against the repair if you go ahead here.

Can I supply my own parts?+

Yes, and we will fit them. The labour warranty stands on its own in that case, because we cannot warrant a part we did not source or inspect.

Do you work on European makes?+

Most of them, and we will tell you honestly when a job needs a dealer tool we do not have rather than take the car apart and find out.

How long will you keep my car?+

Diagnostics are usually same day. Anything needing parts depends on the supplier and you get a time when the report goes out, not after.
