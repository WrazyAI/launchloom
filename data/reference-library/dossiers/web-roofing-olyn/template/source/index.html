Comprehensive Roofing Services in Greater Boston & MetroWest

As a full-service roofing company, O’LYN Roofing is equipped to handle projects of any size or complexity. Whether addressing minor roof repairs or installing a brand-new system, we’re committed to delivering results that stand the test of time. Our services include:

  • Roof Replacement: Upgrade your home with durable and energy-efficient materials tailored to your style and budget.
  • Roof Installation: Expert installation for new construction or additions, ensuring long-lasting protection and performance.
  • Roof Maintenance: Proactive care to extend the life of your roof and prevent costly issues.
  • Roof Repair: Solutions for leaks, storm damage, and other common problems to restore your roof’s integrity.
  • Roof Leak Repair: Swift, targeted repairs to stop water damage and safeguard your property.
  • Roof Ice Melt Systems: Protect your home from winter hazards with advanced ice melt solutions.

Specialized Roofing Options

Every home is unique, and we offer a variety of roofing materials to fit your specific needs and aesthetic preferences:

  • Asphalt Shingle Roofing: A versatile, cost-effective option popular for its durability and variety of designs.
  • Flat Roofing: Ideal for modern homes or commercial properties, offering durability and low-maintenance benefits.
  • Slate Roofing: Timeless elegance and unmatched longevity for a distinctive look.

Gutter Services for a Complete System

Protecting your roof extends beyond the shingles. Our comprehensive gutter services include:

  • Gutter Replacement: Upgrade to a system that effectively manages water runoff and protects your home’s foundation.

Before & After
New Roof in Newton, MA

O’LYN Roofing installed a new Estate Grey Owens Corning roof on this home in Greater Boston. Originally the home had solar panels on it, but our team was able to coordinate with the solar company to remove the panels and ensure a seamless renovation experience for the owners. We also replaced two skylights and installed new Velux solar-powered skylights. Despite the unique complexity of this project, our team went above and beyond for another satisfied customer!

Roof Replacement in Dedham, MA

This cozy Dedham home has a steep roof facing the curb with plenty of passing traffic. The owners knew their roof was at the end of its lifespan, but they also wanted to enhance their home’s aesthetic by trying out a new look. O’LYN Roofing helped them upgrade from a three-tab shingle to a beautiful architectural Owens Corning Duration Designer Shingle. The owners chose a vibrant Aged Copper color which created a dramatic difference in curb appeal. Now when people walk by, they can pick out the gorgeous color combinations of the designer shingle.

Roof Replacement in Waban, MA

This tired roof definitely needed a replacement by the time the owners called O’LYN for an estimate. Located in the suburbs of Greater Boston, this Newton home has a modern and contemporary look in an otherwise historic neighborhood. Our skilled roofing crews were able to complete this roof replacement, upgrade three skylights, and install high-performance ridge ventilation to keep the attic cool and dry. The Owens Corning shingle color, Onyx Black, resulted in a serious enhancement to curb appeal and matched well with the existing siding and garage door.

Church Roof Replacement in Milton, MA

This local Milton church needed its roof replaced promptly and efficiently. O’LYN Roofing completed the project in under two days and upgraded the property’s skylights to solar-powered Velux skylights. Our crew installed an Owens Corning TruDefintion Duration shingle – an affordable yet highly durable product designed to withstand even the toughest New England weather. Overall, the organization was very happy with the final product and timely completion of the installation.
