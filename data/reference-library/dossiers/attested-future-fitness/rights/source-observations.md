# Direct-source observations

- Official direct page captured: https://future.co/
- Observed page title: “Future | Custom, 1-on-1 Personal Training”. The current hero offers a personal-coach proposition and onboarding CTA.
- Business-type distinction: Future is presented as a digital personal-training/coaching service with a companion app and remote coach experience, not as a local physical gym. Use only as a fitness/coaching interaction reference; a generated local-gym page must use the client's actual local business facts and not claim Future's services.
- Observed hierarchy: atmospheric app-led hero and phone mockup; promotional announcement bar; coach/service feature carousel; a personal-coach introduction; a structured 360-degree member roadmap; results/testimonial media; coach discovery and quiz CTA; footer with learning, company, and connection links.
- Observed responsive behavior: mobile stacks the product phone, feature cards, coach roadmap, results, and final coach invitation; the page remained at 390 px wide without horizontal overflow.
- Source rendering issue at capture time: several image elements in the workout-card carousel failed, including Cloudinary-fetched workout assets and empty `src` nodes. See `capture-record.md`.
- Only the direct website screenshot is included. No page asset, image, video, logo, font, script, or gallery thumbnail was separately downloaded or stored.

## Transferable design mechanics

Study the oversized editorial promise, photo-and-product-screen stage, horizontally browsable feature carousel, coach-personalization narrative, structured plan visualization, results section, and repeated onboarding CTA. For a local gym, translate these into a service or class explorer, real trainer/coach facts, practical intake expectations, and local contact details. Do not copy health claims, outcome metrics, coach/member identities, or app screenshots.
