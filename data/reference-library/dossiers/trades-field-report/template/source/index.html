What to know before requesting service.

What details help with a service request?+

Share whether the door is open or closed, what you hear or see, the opener type if known, and the property address.

Should I try to move a door with a damaged spring or cable?+

Avoid operating or lifting a door that appears unstable or has damaged spring or cable hardware. Describe the issue and wait for qualified help.

How is service-area coverage confirmed?+

Provide the full property address. The team will confirm current coverage and availability before scheduling.
