Conversation Piece

Approaching jewelry as an extension of the body, each piece becomes an act and a reaction, shaped by an emotional dialogue with the inner self. Adornment is not passive, but intentional: forms twist and turn, articulating the body in a choreography of movement and presence.

Objects Discover

Composed as a sculptural totem, the Ascolto Candelabra brings together stacked gemstones in a delicate equilibrium between one another. Each element feels both intentional and precarious, creating a sense of movement within stillness. When lit, the flame interacts with the stones, amplifying their depth and color, turning the act of illumination into a quiet, ceremonial gesture.

Objects Discover

Cylindrical metal vases in three sizes animated by gemstones set on fine metal stems that extend outward from the surface. The radiating elements evoke natural forms, stems and blossoms in bloom, while suggesting a constellation of stones in orbit. Balancing structure with a sense of movement, the piece is both functional and sculptural, bringing warmth, color, and a gentle sense of wonder to a space.
