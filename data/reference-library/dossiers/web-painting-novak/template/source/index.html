Frequently Asked

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does exterior paint last in the Phoenix heat? +
In Greater Phoenix, a properly prepped exterior repaint typically holds up 7–10 years. Rushed prep cuts that in half. UV exposure is what breaks paint down here, so we clean and repair the surface, prime bare spots, and use UV-resistant coatings rated for Arizona sun. Prep is where longevity comes from, and it's the step we never skip.
Is refinishing kitchen cabinets cheaper than replacing them? +
Substantially cheaper. A full cabinet replacement can run into the tens of thousands, while refinishing delivers a sharp, factory-fresh finish for a fraction of that cost. If your cabinet boxes are structurally sound, refinishing is almost always the better value.
Can you paint to my HOA's approved color list? +
Yes. HOA color approval is a routine part of exterior work in Phoenix. We match the colors your HOA has approved and keep the finish compliant, so a repaint doesn't turn into a violation letter.
Do you repair stucco cracks before painting? +
We do, and we handle it as one job. Stucco is everywhere in Phoenix and cracks don't wait. Instead of sending you to a second contractor, we repair the stucco and repaint it with one crew, on one call.
Are you insured, and are estimates free? +
Novak Painting Group is bonded and fully insured, and every estimate is free. The owner comes out and quotes the work personally. No call center and no commissioned salesperson. Call 715-223-7083 for a straight answer on what your project takes.
