The ritual

We are more than a barbershop; we provide an experience. Here is what a visit looks like.

  1. Step 1

    The consultation

    Sit down, get offered a drink, and talk it through. Show a photo if you have one. We look at your hair and scalp before anyone picks up a tool.

  2. Step 2

    The cut

    Scissors, clippers or a straight edge, whichever the style calls for. Precision first, then a wash with our own products.

  3. Step 3

    The hot towel

    For beards and shaves: hot towels open the skin, the razor does its work, and a cold finish closes it. Ear flaming as required.

  4. Step 4

    The finish

    Styled, and if it is a new look, a quick tutorial on how to wear it. You leave knowing how to do it again tomorrow.
