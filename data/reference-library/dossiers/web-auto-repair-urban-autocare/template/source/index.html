The Urban Autocare Difference

Since 1993, Urban Autocare has been redefining the auto repair experience for the residents of Metro Denver. Our journey began with a simple mission — to provide an uplifting service experience to every client. We have grown to encompass locations in North Denver, South Denver, Denver Highlands, Lakewood, Littleton, and Arvada, each embodying our ethos of quality, trust, and care.

What sets us apart is our commitment to not just fixing your vehicle but also educating and empowering you as a car owner, as well as employing eco-friendly practices to keep our services as sustainable as possible. At Urban Autocare, your car is cared for by the finest factory-trained Master Technicians, ensuring every service meets our high standards of excellence.

Our History

Your Trusted Auto Excellence Partner in The Denver Area

PREMIUM GRADE PARTS AND TRAINING

Welcome to Urban Autocare where our team has been serving folks here in Denver, Littleton, Lakewood, and Arvada, including residents of City Park, and our local neighborhoods: Park Hill, Washington Park, Downtown, and Uptown. We've been in operation since 1993 (we were formerly Autotailor). We're located within 5 minutes of downtown, our location combines thriving urban businesses with historical residential living. Our rich auto shop history shows a commitment to serving the community of the Denver Metro at the highest level. And while we’ve ushered in a new brand and additional staff to the original namesake, Autotailor, we’re focused on improving upon your previous experience while increasing value for the years ahead.
