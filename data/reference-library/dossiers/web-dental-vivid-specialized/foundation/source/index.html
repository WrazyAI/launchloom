Advanced Dental Care, Led by Specialists

Vivid Specialized Dentistry is a multidisciplinary specialty hub, bringing together prosthodontics, periodontics, and an in-house dental laboratory under one roof. We specialize in Edmonton dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, and advanced reconstructive dentistry.

Our multispecialty centre collaborates with leading dentists and specialists across Edmonton and the surrounding area, managing every detail to deliver beautiful, long-lasting smiles for our patients.

We treat patients from across Canada and around the globe, requiring restoration or replacement of teeth.

Your Questions,

Answered

What is a prosthodontist?
A prosthodontist is a dental specialist with advanced training in restoring and replacing teeth, including crowns, bridges, dentures, full-mouth reconstructions, and dental implants. As a prosthodontist & dental implant specialist in Edmonton, Dr. Arif Sumar focuses on complex cases that require precise planning, long-term durability, and highly aesthetic results.
How is a prosthodontist different from a general dentist?

While general dentists provide routine dental care, prosthodontists complete additional years of specialty training focused on complex restorative and implant dentistry. At Vivid Specialized Dentistry, this means your treatment is planned with architectural precision, prioritizing function, longevity, and aesthetics, especially for advanced or previously unsuccessful cases.

Why choose a multidisciplinary dental specialty clinic in Edmonton?
Choosing a multidisciplinary clinic allows all aspects of your care: prosthodontics, periodontics, implant surgery, and laboratory fabrication to happen under one roof. This integrated approach reduces errors, improves communication, shortens treatment timelines, and delivers more predictable, long-lasting outcomes.
Do I need a referral to see a dental implant specialist?
No referral is required. Patients can book directly with our team. We also work closely with referring dentists across Alberta and Canada for collaborative care, second opinions, and advanced treatment planning.
What types of cases do you treat?

As Edmonton’s dental implant and cosmetic dentistry specialists, we commonly treat:

 

  • Failing crowns, bridges, and implants
  • Missing teeth or severe tooth wear
  • Full-mouth reconstructions
    All-on-x / Teeth in a Day® treatments
  • Trauma, cancer-related, or congenital dental cases
  • Complex cosmetic dentistry requiring specialist planning

 

If you’ve been told your case is “too complex,” this is exactly where a prosthodontist-led approach matters.

What makes Vivid an Edmonton Cosmetic Dental Specialist?
Cosmetic dentistry at Vivid goes far beyond surface aesthetics. As an Edmonton cosmetic dental specialist practice, every smile is designed with bite balance, facial harmony, and long-term health in mind, combining digital smile design, advanced materials, and in-house laboratory precision to achieve natural, enduring results.
What happens during a new patient examination?

Your initial visit includes a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health, bite, existing dental work, and aesthetic concerns. Depending on your needs, advanced diagnostics such as digital scans or 3D imaging may be recommended. You’ll then receive a personalized treatment plan with clear options, always with informed consent and no pressure to proceed.

Do you offer financing for dental implants and smile makeovers?
Yes. We offer 0% financing options and long-term payment plans through Beautifi to make comprehensive care more accessible. Our team will walk you through available options during your consultation.
How long do dental implants and restorations last?
With proper planning, materials, and maintenance, implant restorations and prosthetics can last decades. Our specialist-led planning, in-house lab fabrication, and comprehensive hygiene protocols are designed to maximize longevity, making quality dentistry more cost-effective over time.
Are you accepting patients from outside Edmonton?
Yes. Patients travel to our Edmonton clinic from across Alberta, Canada, and internationally for advanced dental implant and cosmetic care. Our team helps coordinate care efficiently for out-of-town patients whenever possible.
How do I know if I need a prosthodontist or implant specialist?
If you’ve experienced repeated dental failures, have multiple missing teeth, need full-mouth treatment, or want long-lasting cosmetic results, seeing a prosthodontist & dental implant specialist in Edmonton ensures your care is planned correctly from the start.
