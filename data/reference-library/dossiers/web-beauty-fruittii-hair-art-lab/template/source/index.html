THIS IS FRUITTII

Fruittii is more than a hair salon.

It’s a feeling. A release. A return to yourself.

We are an inclusive, LGBTQIA+ -affirming, gender-neutral hair and barber social lab — built for expression, individuality, and care. A space where every identity is respected, every story is valid, and you are seen without explanation, heard without judgment, and held in complete confidence.

What Makes Fruittii Inclusive
London • since 2014

Here, hair is never just hair

It’s identity. Energy. Story. Our Hair stylists work intuitively and collaboratively, listening deeply to who you are and how you want to feel. Every cut, colour, and transformation is created with intention,to elevate your confidence, enhance your natural beauty, and leave you feeling lighter, clearer, and more aligned. This is a place to arrive as you are… and leave feeling renewed. A hairstyle you didn’t just want, but believed in.
