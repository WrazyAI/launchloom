# The Uncommon Founder principles atlas: archive-only design implementation brief

This document extracts visual mechanics from the permission-cleared source captures. It is not source copy and does not grant permission to reuse any source brand asset. The dossier is archive-only and is not eligible for automatic production selection.

## visual hierarchy
Create a founder-led advisory narrative that feels reflective, specific, and self-possessed. Use a charcoal field, white/gray type, one pale-lime signal, and restrained mono micro-labels. Open with a centered promise and sparse particle/line atmosphere, not a split hero. Give the page a clear principles framework, a short logo/proof pause only when verified, an essay-like founder point of view, differentiated client needs or process stages, an expertise choice, and a large conversation close. Keep the typography-led rhythm and negative space, but make every pause intentional and content-bearing.

## page sequence
Use: compact wordmark/nav; centered promise; verified trust strip; principles or method; short narrative; contextual service choices; permissioned proof or a process demonstration; partnership steps; expertise/service decision; one signal-color CTA; optional secondary newsletter; concise footer. The story should help the visitor decide if the advisory offer fits.

## responsive translation
At 390px, stack framework and proof cards, keep prose narrow and readable, and turn hover details into visible or tap-accessible content. Maintain one primary conversation CTA. Decorative particles become static or disappear for reduced motion and must not affect contrast or page width.

## signature elements
Required mechanics: a sparse pixel/point field, a founder-specific principle sequence, differentiated expertise choices, and a high-contrast conversation close. Rebuild the ideas from client evidence; use new names, original copy, and permissioned proof only.

## prohibited patterns
Never copy The Uncommon Founder story, framework language, brand, client logos, images, testimonials, quotes, results, or exact green treatment. No generic agency cards, fake client wins, invented founder credentials, or claim that all clients achieve the same outcome. Preserve local SEO facts and make the CTA go to a verified channel.

## local-business translation and SEO
For a LaunchLoom client, translate this composition only after grounding the page in the client's verified business kind, services, service area, customer questions, contact channels, assets, and SEO research. Keep the opening statement useful to the actual searcher, use natural service/location language, answer the real decision questions, and place one primary conversion action early. Never copy source copy, logos, people, photos, products, claims, or interface assets. Any missing fact remains absent or is requested from the client; do not fill it with a plausible invention. Keep semantic headings, keyboard and touch access, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and a working form or call action.

### Type, surfaces and image roles

- Display character: modern geometric sans with pixel/mono micro-labels. Body character: readable neutral sans. Hierarchy: large centered promise, medium essay lines, tiny labels used as navigation rather than body text. Translate these roles to the host's local font catalog and honor client-selected heading/body families. Specify a responsive scale, line-height and intentional wrapping; keep body lines under about 80 characters. Do not copy source font files or fetch remote fonts.
- Source surface cues: graphite; near black; slightly raised charcoal. Contrast intent: lime marks selected ideas and conversion; body copy remains white/gray with accessible contrast. Propose 4-6 named client-compatible color tokens with hex values in the contract, labeled as implementation choices, not measured source colors. Bind them to the host's accessible per-surface palette roles rather than overriding client brand variables.
- Media role: sparse pixel particles, restrained founder/client evidence, and minimal line symbols. Crop guidance: mostly typographic sections with a few inset video/proof cards. Focal role: the principle or client progress story currently being explained. Map opening/secondary/detail placements to the available client image tokens. Preserve role, focal point and mobile crop without using reference screenshots as site assets. If the right media is absent, use an allowed contextual asset or deliberate graphic treatment, and flag any signature that cannot be met; never imply stock people or spaces belong to the client.
- Service presentation: expertise options as three distinct advisory roles supported by principle and process. Early action: small conversation action in the header/hero; large CTA follows the expertise explanation. Primary placement: Book or request a verified introductory conversation. Name the actual client next step; repeat it at decision points, not mechanically after every chapter.

### Mobile and interaction translation

Recorded mobile strategy: **single-column reflective essay with stacked proof and process**. keep the centered promise concise; stack the principles and client-progress cards with clear labels; remove hover-only pixel effects; preserve generous but not empty vertical rhythm; keep the lime CTA full-width and readable; do not present client logos or testimonials without verified consent.

Record separately what stacks, reorders, changes crop, wraps, becomes a menu or becomes a touch control. Do not just shrink desktop. Keep the primary action, visible focus, labels and form controls usable without hover or motion; no horizontal overflow or competing sticky overlays. Recorded interaction opportunity: slow, sparse pixel-field drift and a scroll-led sequence that marks principles/progress. Reduced-motion contract: freeze particle field and show all principles and client evidence in natural order. Implement only the justified documented interaction, with equivalent static content. Do not add repeated fade-up entrances, autoplay or section pinning to compensate for an unclear layout.

### Plan, build and critique

Before code, use the existing contract stage to state the client's audience and primary task, choose the token system, draw desktop/mobile wireframes, map chapters to content, and name one memorable reference-led element. Check against this family's composition and prohibited patterns; revise any choice that could belong to an unrelated generic page. Source-required cream/serif, dark accents, uppercase navigation, cards or rules are legitimate when they serve this specific reference, not automatic reasons to redesign it.

Build from the revised contract. Then inspect rendered desktop, compact desktop and mobile, including menu/form/disclosure states. Compare actual geometry, section rhythm, image roles and every signature with the paired references, not just DOM markers. Verify contrast, working calls/links/forms, keyboard focus, reduced motion, truthful content and no em dashes in rendered copy. Remove decoration that does not serve the brief. Report any unmet signature or unavailable evidence; do not claim rendered fidelity from a successful build alone.
