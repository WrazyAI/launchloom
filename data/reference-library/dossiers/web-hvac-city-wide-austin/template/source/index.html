Repair or replace?

Make the decision with the full picture.

There is no one-size-fits-all answer. The right choice depends on the system and your priorities.

Repair may fit when
  • The system is otherwise reliable
  • The problem is isolated and repairable
  • Parts are reasonably available
Replacement may fit when
  • Breakdowns are becoming frequent
  • Comfort or energy use is a concern
  • The equipment is near the end of its useful life
Explore both options
