The Cat Clinic difference

01

Feline-only expertise

Our vets have decades of experience caring exclusively for cats, so your companion is always in knowledgeable, cat-focused hands.

02

A little about us

Excellence in feline care, delivered without fuss

The Cat Clinic is an independent veterinary practice in Edinburgh, dedicated exclusively to cats.

We believe cats deserve care that respects their nature - unhurried, thoughtful and grounded in deep understanding. Our team quietly advocates for every cat in our care, while supporting their humans with honesty, empathy and clear guidance.

We don’t shout about being the best. We simply show up, listen carefully, and do what’s right, for cats, for clients and for each other.
