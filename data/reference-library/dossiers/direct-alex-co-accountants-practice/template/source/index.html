Who we are

A small firm that treats your business like its own

Alex and Co was formed with one simple aim: to give small and medium sized businesses, charities and individuals the kind of accountancy support that is usually reserved for much larger organisations. That means proper management information, honest tax advice and accounts that are ready well before the deadline, all at a cost that makes sense for a smaller business.

When you work with us you deal directly with the person doing your work. There is no call centre and no being passed from department to department. We get to know how your business actually runs, because that is the only way to give advice that is genuinely useful rather than generic.

How we work

A clear process, with no surprises

01

We listen first

Everything starts with a free, no obligation conversation about your situation, your worries and what you want your business to do. You talk, we listen, and we will tell you honestly whether and how we can help.

02

We agree the work and the fee

Before anything begins, you receive a clear proposal setting out exactly what we will do, when we will do it and what it will cost. The fee is fixed at this point, so there are no surprises later.

03

We do the work and keep you informed

We prepare your accounts, returns or reports within the agreed timescale and flag anything important as we go, including tax saving opportunities and deadlines that are coming up.

04

We explain it properly

Numbers are only useful if you understand them. We sit down with you, in person or on the phone, and walk through what your figures are actually saying about your business.

Why choose us

Why businesses stay with us

Most of our clients come to us after a bad experience elsewhere: accountants who never called back, surprise bills, or accounts that arrived the day before the deadline. We built this firm to be the opposite of that.

We answer the phone, including in the evenings, because we know that is when most business owners finally have a moment to think about their finances. We agree our fees before we start. We file on time, every time, and we remind you about deadlines instead of waiting for you to chase us. And because we also prepare management accounts, we often spot tax savings and cash flow problems months before a year end accountant would ever see them.
