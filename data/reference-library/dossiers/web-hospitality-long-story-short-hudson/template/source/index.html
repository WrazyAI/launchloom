A private stay in the heart of Hudson.

Long Story Short is a four-bedroom petite hotel on Warren Street, offered entirely to one group at a time. Three floors, six beds, a private garden, and room for up to twelve, creating the rare luxury of guests having an entire space to themselves.

No shared walls. No strangers in the hallway. Just the people you came with.
